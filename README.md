AquaIntel – Mobile-Based Domain-Specific AI for Smart Aquarium Management

AquaIntel is a mobile-based intelligent aquarium management system designed to simplify and automate fish tank care through a domain-restricted AI chatbot and smart monitoring tools. The application focuses exclusively on aquarium-related knowledge, ensuring accurate and relevant responses for fish care, tank maintenance, and water quality management.

At the core of the system is a domain-restricted AI chatbot, which is specifically trained to handle aquarium-related queries only. Unlike general AI systems, this chatbot filters out unrelated topics and provides precise, reliable guidance on fish species, feeding habits, disease prevention, and tank conditions.

The application also includes a smart reminder and notification system, helping users maintain a healthy aquarium environment. It sends timely alerts for:

Fish feeding schedules
Water changes
Filter cleaning and maintenance

This reduces manual tracking and ensures consistent care routines.

AquaIntel features an interactive dashboard that allows users to monitor and manage their aquarium efficiently. The dashboard provides:

1. Real-time tracking of tank conditions
2. pH level monitoring
3. Tank details and history logs
4. Chatbot restricted to Fish Related only

By combining AI, automation, and user-friendly mobile design, AquaIntel transforms traditional aquarium management into a smart, data-driven experience. The system not only improves fish well-being but also reduces user effort, making it ideal for both beginners and experienced aquarium owners.
